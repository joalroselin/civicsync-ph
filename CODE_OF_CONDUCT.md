# Code of Conduct

CivicSync PH is a civic project meant to serve everyone, whatever their
politics, region, language, background, or level of experience. We want
contributing to feel the same way.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, including "basic" ones.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Keep discussions about the project. This isn't a venue for campaigning
  for or against any politician, party, or group.
- Respect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information without consent.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery
- Deliberately spreading false information about the project, its data, or
  other people
- Encouraging any of the above

## Where this applies

In all project spaces, including GitHub issues and pull requests, email, and
any community channels, and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email hello.joaldev@gmail.com with the subject "Conduct report: CivicSync PH".
Reports are handled privately. We may respond by asking someone to change
their behaviour, removing content, or blocking people from the project,
depending on what happened.
